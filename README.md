## About Me

I'm an Electrical Engineer with ~12 years of experience in renewable energy operations, specializing in solar project management, open access regulations, and group captive compliance across Maharashtra and Uttar Pradesh. Over the years, I've navigated complex regulatory frameworks (MERC, UPERC, MSEDCL), managed energy settlements, and worked closely with the operational and commercial side of solar power.

I'm now channeling that domain depth into data engineering, analytics, and AI — building hands-on skills in Microsoft Fabric, Databricks, and Power BI, while working toward certifications like DP-600, DP-700, and Databricks Data Engineer Associate. My goal is to bridge deep energy-sector knowledge with modern data and AI tooling, bringing a perspective that's rare in either world alone.

I'm actively exploring opportunities in clean energy, SaaS, and industrial sectors where domain expertise and data fluency intersect — from energy analytics and forecasting to AI-driven operational tools.

**Currently focused on:** Fabric/Databricks pipelines, Generative & Agentic AI fundamentals, and applying data engineering practices to energy use cases.

## What I Do

- **Energy Operations & Compliance** — Manage solar project operations, open access approvals, and group captive compliance across Maharashtra and Uttar Pradesh, working within MERC, UPERC, and MSEDCL regulatory frameworks.
- **Regulatory & Commercial Analysis** — Handle energy loss settlements, curtailment exposure, banking restrictions, and RPO obligations, translating regulatory complexity into actionable operational decisions.
- **Data & AI Upskilling** — Build hands-on expertise in Microsoft Fabric and Databricks (pipelines, Lakehouse architecture, orchestration), and apply Power BI for advanced dashboarding and analysis.
- **AI-Driven Problem Solving** — Learn and apply Generative AI and Agentic AI concepts (LangChain, RAG, CrewAI, AutoGen) to explore how AI can solve real energy-sector challenges like forecasting and operational automation.
- **Bridging Domain & Technology** — Combine 14 years of energy-sector depth with emerging data and AI skills to help energy, SaaS, and industrial organizations make smarter, data-backed decisions.
